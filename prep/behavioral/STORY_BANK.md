# Story Bank (Behavioral)

Target: **9–12 stories**, each mappable to ≥ 2 Amazon Leadership Principles. Every story ends with a **measured result** and, ideally, a lesson. As flagship milestones land, add those stories — they're your freshest material.

## Story template (STARL)

- **S**ituation: 2 sentences max — team, system, stakes
- **T**ask: what YOU owned (ownership pronouns: I, not we)
- **A**ction: 3–5 specific actions incl. one trade-off decision and one data point
- **R**esult: numbers (latency, cost, error rate, adoption, revenue)
- **L**esson: what you'd do differently / what you learned
